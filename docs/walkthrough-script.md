# Walkthrough Script

1. **Open `/`.** Confirm the app redirects directly into the Desk systems catalog view, with no marketing homepage in between.
2. **Open `/catalog`.** Show the complete twelve-product catalog, filter rail, search, sort, and grid/list controls.
3. **Search `TL-ST-422`.** Confirm SKU lookup returns Route Archive Crate; clear and combine category/availability filters.
4. **Open a product.** Review gallery, SKU, material, finish, dimensions, lead time, MOQ, three tiers, and related products.
5. **Change quantity and add.** Show price selection and the shared header/drawer count.
6. **Refresh.** Confirm the order draft remains available in the same browser.
7. **Review `/order`.** Demonstrate below-MOQ protection, quantity updates, and field-level validation.
8. **Prepare request.** Explain the explicit boundary: the app validates data and creates a reference but sends no email, collects no payment, and creates no external record.
9. **Reload the result.** Confirm the request summary, contact details, product totals, notes, and reference remain stable locally.
10. **Finish at the footer.** Show functional print, order-draft, category, and back-to-top actions plus service/freight disclosures.
11. **Close.** Mention the 12 static product routes, shared business rules, nine tests, production build, and responsive browser QA at 390–1920px.
