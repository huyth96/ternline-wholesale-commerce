# Ternline Brand Brief

## Working brand

**Ternline** — workplace systems, ordered with clarity.

An exact-name collision scan was performed on 2026-08-16. No obvious active workplace-supply business using “Ternline” surfaced. This is a preliminary portfolio check, not a trademark clearance.

## Positioning

Ternline supplies modular, repeatable workplace accessories to professional buyers. Copy should sound operational and specific: case quantities, lead times, materials, finish consistency, and reorder clarity.

## Visual system

- **Canvas:** Warm Chalk `#F6F1E8` and Paper `#FFFDFA`.
- **Primary text:** Carbon `#17211E`.
- **Secondary text:** Slate `#5A6560`.
- **Primary accent:** Harbor navy `#123849` with dark field `#092B3A`.
- **Action accent:** Friendly terracotta `#D86B45`.
- **Supporting fields:** Pale celadon `#DCE9DF` and butter `#F0D98C`.
- **Operational accent:** Signal amber `#D98A35` for low-stock information only.
- **Positive:** Pine `#28705A`.
- **Borders:** Mineral `#D9D9D1`.
- **Typography:** Local system sans stack, with condensed/uppercase treatment for operational labels. No external font dependency.
- **Spacing:** 4px base; primary rhythm 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64.
- **Radii:** 2px controls, 4px media; pills only for compact statuses.
- **Shadows:** One restrained drawer shadow; cards rely on borders and background shift.
- **Imagery:** Neutral studio product photography on warm gray surfaces, three-quarter angle, consistent 4:3 card crops.
- **Wordmark:** Code-native TERNLINE wordmark with a three-rail SVG mark and one terracotta routing node, derived from the approved transparent logo reference.

## Tone of voice

- Concise, practical, and informed.
- Prefer “Ships in 2–3 business days” over “Fast shipping.”
- Prefer “Powder-coated steel; replaceable cork feet” over abstract benefit claims.
- Never imply an email, request, or purchase was completed without a real integration.

## Layout direction

The catalog uses a full-width application shell: a slim utility bar, compact header, restrained catalog title band, dense filter rail, and a 3-column product field at 1440px. Product cards expose operational data immediately. The top behaves like procurement software rather than a marketing hero. The bottom closes with real ordering context and working catalog actions rather than slogans, newsletter capture, or unfinished navigation. Mobile keeps 48px controls, moves filters to a drawer, and uses one-column cards.
